## 要約

縦 9:16 の4コマ画像。赤茶髪ハーフアップの女性が、赤いハート柄のピンクの部屋着で、手の形と背景の大文字で「LOVE」を表現する。

コマ順（手の形）:

1. **L** — 親指 + 人差し指
2. **O** — OKサイン
3. **V** — ピース
4. **E** — 口元を隠すポーズ

## プロンプト（優希さんの作例に合わせた調整版）

```text
Vertical 9:16, a 4-panel photo collage (2x2 grid) of the same young woman
with reddish-brown hair in a half-up style, wearing pink pajamas with a
red heart pattern. In each panel she forms one letter of "LOVE" with her
hands, with a large matching letter in the background:

Panel 1 "L": thumb and index finger forming an L shape.
Panel 2 "O": an OK hand sign forming an O.
Panel 3 "V": a peace sign forming a V.
Panel 4 "E": hand covering her mouth, playful expression.

Same character, same outfit and same room in all four panels, bright
cheerful lighting, photorealistic, cute and lively mood.
```
