```text
[Camera / Composition]
85mm lens, f/2, chest-high angle, subject placed right of center,
asymmetric composition.

[Pose]
Chin raised, eyes closed, face in profile, elegant and confident.

[Styling]
Cream-colored fur coat, gold earrings, glamorous makeup.

[Light / Color / Finish]
Warm side lighting, visible skin pore texture, photorealistic.
Color palette (HEX): #262B20, #181B14, #EFE8DA, #D6C7B2, #D4AF37,
#E2B496, #3B261D, #B87466.
Negative: no blur, no distortion, no cartoon look.
```
